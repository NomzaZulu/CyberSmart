You've been scammed. Now what?

Start with these four steps. If you're in danger, get help from a trusted adult immediately.

01
Stop

Stop replying. Don't click more links, send money, or share more information.

02
Protect

Secure affected accounts through the official app or website. If money is involved, contact your bank promptly.

03
Get help

Tell a trusted adult and keep relevant messages or screenshots if it is safe to do so.

04
Report

Use the appropriate official reporting service. For financial cyber fraud in India, call 1930 promptly.
